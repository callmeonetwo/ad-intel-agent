# Women's swimwear, DTC fashion (UK): competitor creative report

*Snapshot 2026-10-22 · 60 active ads from 20 advertisers · 14 brand reels · built by Ad Intel Agent*

**UK swimwear competitors that have run for a long time lean on plain catalog (DPA) ads with a clear product or fit promise rather than discounts, while Triangl's Reels show that short, original-audio lifestyle videos around 15-20 seconds get the most reach.**

- Among the 25 direct-brand ads, 20 have run 90+ days (median 182 days). 16 of the 25 are catalog (DPA) ads, which likely signals a steady, profitable format.
- Discounts are not what the long runners rely on: ads with no offer have a median of 237 days live, versus 163 for discount ads (likely, not proven).
- Triangl's 14 own Reels have a median of 79,976 views. 15-20s Reels do best (median 92,078 views, n=7), and 85.7% use original audio.

## Key findings

### 1. Catalog (DPA) ads dominate and last longest among brands
Most direct-brand ads are automated catalog ads that pull product photos from a feed. These are likely the steady earners because advertisers keep them on. Newer ads lean more on dynamic creative (DCO), which is not yet proven.

*Evidence:* DPA 16 of 25 brand ads, median 222 days live. 75% of 90+ day ads are catalog/DPA vs 20% of newer ones. DCO median 66 days (n=4), 0% of 90+ day ads, 80% of newer ads.

### 2. Discounts are not the long-run driver
Ads with no offer have lived longer than ads with a discount. Days live is only a proxy, but it suggests product and fit messages can carry an ad without a price cut. Guarantee ads are the longest of the offer types, but only 2 ads, so directional.

*Evidence:* Offer median days: guarantee 268 (n=2), none 237 (n=17), discount 163 (n=5). 17 of 25 brand ads have no offer. 0% use urgency.

### 3. A clear promise or benefit list beats a plain offer hook
Hooks that state a specific promise or list benefits have lived longer than offer-led hooks. The promise group is tiny, so treat it as a hint. Statements are the most common hook.

*Evidence:* Median days live: promise 834 (n=2), benefit_list 237 (n=4), statement 206 (n=13), offer 152 (n=6). Statement is 13 of 25 brand ads.

### 4. Fit and body-specific messaging is how Bravissimo holds ground
Bravissimo runs five different copies about bigger-bust support and perfect fit, with free returns and exchanges in some. They have run a median of 237 days. Tummy control also appears across other brands.

*Evidence:* Bravissimo: 5 ads, 5 unique copies, median 237 days, max 330, 2 with guarantee. 'Tummy control' angle: 2 ads. 'Bigger bust support': 1 ad.

### 5. Social proof is rare in long runners and more common in newer ads
Few long-running ads use social proof, but 2 of the 5 newer brand ads do (a small group, so directional). Brands may be testing it now. It is not yet proven.

*Evidence:* Social proof: 5.0% of 90+ day ads vs 40.0% of newer still-running ads (n=20 vs n=5). Overall brand share 12.0%.

### 6. Triangl Reels: 15-20s and original audio are the sweet spot
Reels of 15-20 seconds got the highest median views. Offer or promo captions did not lift views. Licensed audio shows higher median views, but only 2 Reels, so directional.

*Evidence:* Views by length: <15s 54,114 (n=5), 15-20s 92,078 (n=7), 20s+ 78,024 (n=2). Promo caption 73,724 (n=3) vs no offer 82,323 (n=11). Original audio 75,677 (n=12) vs licensed 139,646 (n=2). Median engagement 0.86%.

### 7. Marketplaces run template catalog ads, a different game
SHEIN and similar run automated catalog ads with templated copy. This is a volume play, not a creative model to copy. Their ads are also mostly newer (SHEIN median 23 days).

*Evidence:* 31 of 60 ads are marketplace. Template copy share 93.5%. SHEIN: 22 ads, all DPA, 95.5% template copy, median 23 days.

## Who sells how

- **Bravissimo**: Sells fit and comfort for bigger busts through catalog ads, with free returns and exchanges.
- **Triangl Swimwear**: Runs dynamic creative ads (summer swimwear, new colours, buy now pay later) and posts original-audio lifestyle Reels with #TrianglGirls.
- **BellabarnettSwimwear**: Long-running catalog ads on new arrivals and 24-hour shipping, backed by '100k customers'.
- **Gianelle**: Luxury holiday framing ('Forever Vacay') in catalog ads.
- **Inalbis**: Simple image ads inviting people to see bikinis and follow for summer vibes.
- **Innovare Denim**: Discount-led catalog ads, including seasonal discounts and codes.
- **Sunny bee**: Video ad focused on tummy control.
- **DOLL Swimwear**: Premium swimwear positioning in one catalog ad.

## Gaps nobody is using well

- Creator or UGC-style video ads: only 1 influencer ad among 25 brand ads, yet a creator collab Reel for Triangl hit 678,127 views (8.48x the median).
- Social proof and reviews: only 12.0% of brand ads use them, and no brand ad pairs fit claims with customer proof.
- Urgency messaging: 0% of brand ads use it, so there is open space for seasonal deadlines, though it is untested.
- Video: only 3 of 25 brand ads are video, so there is little competition in motion creative for the UK 25-40 audience.

## Recommendations: what to make next

### 1. Launch a fit-led catalog set (start now)
**Make:** Build a DPA catalog template with 3-4 copy variants about fit and support in different body types (bust, tummy, bottoms). Keep each copy to two lines with one benefit list. Include a returns line.

**Why:** Bravissimo's 5 fit-led ads have a median of 237 days live. Benefit_list hooks median 237 days (n=4). Guarantee offers median 268 days (n=2, directional).

**Example hook:** "Swimwear that fits your shape: support, shaping and free returns if it's not right."

### 2. Shoot 15-20s original-audio lifestyle Reels (start now)
**Make:** A batch of 6-8 vertical Reels, 15-20 seconds each, shot at the pool, hotel and beach. Use native audio, one outfit change, and a short caption with a branded hashtag.

**Why:** 15-20s Reels had a median of 92,078 views (n=7) vs 54,114 for under 15s (n=5). 85.7% of Triangl Reels use original audio.

**Example hook:** "Pool day, sorted. ☀️ Three pieces, one bag."

### 3. Brief a creator collab with a humour angle (test)
**Make:** One creator-led Reel of around 30-60 seconds where the creator pokes fun at making content for the brand. Use a trending licensed sound, and let the creator style and choreograph it.

**Why:** The jayneejaynee collab for Triangl reached 678,127 views, 8.48x the median. It is a single example, so test it.

**Example hook:** "When the marketing team ask me to make content, this is what they get 😂"

### 4. Test a no-discount, price-fair message (test)
**Make:** A catalog and static pair: one with a value message at your price point (quality close to premium, price below it), one with a discount. Run both against the same audience.

**Why:** No-offer ads have a median of 237 days vs 163 for discount ads (n=17 vs 5). 17 of 25 brand ads have no offer.

**Example hook:** "Premium-feel swimwear without the premium price tag."

### 5. Add a review-led static and video series (test)
**Make:** Make 3 static ads and 1 short video quoting real UK customer reviews, with a star line and the product shown. Keep one review per ad.

**Why:** Social proof appears in only 12.0% of brand ads but in 40.0% of newer ones (n=5). Bellabarnett uses '100k customers' on its longest-running ad (1,487 days).

**Example hook:** "'Finally a bikini that stays put.' Rated by UK customers like you."

### 6. Make a fabric or fit education Reel (later)
**Make:** A 25-30 second explainer Reel about fabric, lining or sizing, with a question hook and on-screen text.

**Why:** Triangl's fabric education Reel (30.2s) got 82,323 views, right at the median (1.03x). Only one example, so directional.

**Example hook:** "Not sure what size to pick? Here's what you should know."

### 7. Test a seasonal-deadline message (later)
**Make:** One static and one catalog variant with a clear date, such as a winter sun or holiday dispatch cut-off. Keep it factual.

**Why:** 0% of brand ads use urgency, so it is untested in this set. Only run it if the deadline is real.

**Example hook:** "Order by Friday to have your winter sun swimwear packed in time."

## Limits of this data

- Every ad in the snapshot is still running, so days live is a proxy for success, not proof. Newer ads are not failures.
- Several groups are small: promise hooks (n=2), guarantee offers (n=2), image ads (n=2), and newer ads (n=5). Treat them as directional.
- The Reels data is almost all Triangl (14 of 15 are Triangl; 2 other accounts were not found), so it shows one brand's pattern, not the whole market.
- No spend, sales or click data is in the stats, so we cannot say which ads made money.

## Method

Apify collected public ads from the Meta Ad Library and public reels from Instagram. Claude Haiku tagged every creative (hook, promise, angle, emotion, format, proof, urgency, offer, CTA) as JSON; Claude Sonnet wrote the findings from counted statistics only. Full tagged table: `swimwear_uk_creatives.csv`. Model usage for this run: labeling 75 creatives with Haiku: 6 calls, $0.008 (cached after the first run); report with Sonnet: $0.058.
