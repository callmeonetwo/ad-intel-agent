Case study · 2026

Ad Intel Agent

A media buyer spends hours scrolling the Ad Library to work out what competitors run and why. This agent does that research end to end and hands back a short report with recommendations a creative team can brief the same day.

The task

Given a niche, a country and a list of brands, find out which ads and Reels are working for competitors, what they have in common, which formats nobody uses yet, and turn that into 5–7 concrete creative recommendations. Use only public data and stay honest about what the data can and cannot prove.

The approach

  1. CollectApify pulls active ads from the Meta Ad Library and Reels from Instagram.
  2. TagClaude Haiku labels each creative as JSON: hook, promise, angle, emotion, format, proof, urgency, offer, CTA.
  3. ScoreAds by days live, a proxy for payback. Reels by views against the account median.
  4. Find patternsPlain Python counting, so every number in the report can be traced.
  5. ReportClaude Sonnet writes findings and recommendations from the counts. HTML and Markdown with charts.

Marketplaces such as SHEIN and Alibaba run automated catalog ads, so the agent analyses them apart from direct competitors. Each run is saved, and the next weekly run reports what changed.

What the agent found

Women's swimwear · UK

  • 31 of 60 ads came from marketplaces (SHEIN alone: 28). The agent set them aside and focused on the 25 ads from direct competitors.
  • 20 of 25 brand ads have run 90+ days. Ads with no offer have a median of 237 days live, discount ads 163: discounts are not what keeps ads running.
  • No direct competitor uses urgency (0%), and social proof appears in only 12% of brand ads.
  • Triangl Reels: median 79,976 views. 15–20 s clips reach a median of 92,078, under 15 s 54,114. A creator collab hit 678,127, 8.5× the brand median.

AI tools and apps · US

  • 120 ads from 38 advertisers, but only 61 unique copies. Nexus runs 35 ads from 5 texts.
  • Static images run longest: median 224 days, against 79 for video and 29 for dynamic creative.
  • Template copy fills 75% of newer ads but only 9% of ads live 90+ days. Free trials appear in 46 ads, nearly all recent.
  • Social proof shows up in 20% of long-running ads and 8% of newer ones; overall it is underused.

Stack and limits

  • Days live is a proxy for payback, since Meta does not publish spend or results for most commercial ads.
  • Tags come from ad copy and format data. Image and video analysis is the next step.
  • Samples are small, and the reports mark small groups as directional. Findings are research, not a guarantee of results.
